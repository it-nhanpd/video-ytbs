Tactical military operation map of Omaha Beach Normandy 1944. Bird's eye view parchment paper map detailing the invasion sectors: Charlie, Dog Green, Dog White, Easy Green, Easy Red, Fox Green, with five exit draws marked. The English Channel at the top, sand tidal beach in the middle, and coastal bluffs at the bottom. Vintage hand-drawn military cartography style of The Armchair Historian, muted olive green, sandy beige, and pale naval blue tones, 16:9 wide ratio, no modern digital glow.
